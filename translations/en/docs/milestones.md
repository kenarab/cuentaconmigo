# Milestones and board

Author's rule (31/10/2026):

- **Fortnights:** one milestone every 15 days until the end of the 2027 electoral cycle.
- **Each election is a special milestone** (🗳️), as are the key dates of the
  electoral calendar (📅).
- **Margin:** each milestone is due **3 days before** the actual date, to leave
  time if the goal was not reached. The title carries the actual date; the due date,
  the date with margin.
- The milestones of the 31/10/2026 hackathon carry the prefix "Hackatón".
- **Focus: the national election.** Provincial elections may be considered,
  but they are optional and carry "(provincial, opcional)" in the title.
- The dates of the 2027 calendar are **to be verified** against the official schedule of the
  National Electoral Chamber (Cámara Nacional Electoral). Whether the 2027 PASO primaries
  (open, simultaneous and mandatory primaries) will be held is under debate.

Board: [GitHub Project "CuentaConmigo · Hackatón 31/10"](https://github.com/users/kenarab/projects/2)
with the fields **Estado** (Status: Pendiente/Pending, En curso/In progress, Bloqueado/Blocked,
Hecho/Done) and **Tipo** (Type: Tarea/Task, Decisión/Decision). Tasks are issues with the
label `tarea`; decisions, with `decision-humana`.
